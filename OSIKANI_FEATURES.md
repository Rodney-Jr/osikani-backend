# Osikani Features & User Guide

Welcome to **Osikani**, the AI-powered financial literacy assistant for the informal sector in Ghana. This document details every feature tailored to help users manage money, save better, and access credit.

---

## 1. AI Financial Assistant (The Core)
**Access:**  
Click the **Green "O" Button** at the bottom-right of the website (Floating Widget).

**Description:**  
A friendly, context-aware chatbot powered by Gemini 2.0 Flash. It speaks "Ghanaian English" and understands local context (e.g., "susu", "market days").

**How Users Use It:**
- **Onboarding:** When you first chat, Osikani asks for your **Name**, **Business** (e.g., "Kofi, Mason"), and **Location**. This builds your profile.
- **Financial Advice:** Ask questions like *"How do I separate my shop money from my pocket money?"* or *"What is the best way to save for school fees?"*.
- **Context Awareness:** It remembers who you are and tailors advice to your business type.

---

## 2. Voice-Activated Tools (Via Chat)
Users perform complex financial tasks simply by talking to the bot. No forms to fill!

### A. Transaction Logging (Bookkeeping)
**How to Use:**  
Tell the bot: *"I sold 200 cedis of tomatoes today"* or *"I bought fuel for 50 GHS"*.
**What Happens:**  
- The AI detects if it's **Income** or **Expense**.
- It saves the record to your database.
- It confirms: *"Saved! You made 200 GHS. Good sales o!"*

### B. Loan Readiness Scoring
**How to Use:**  
Ask: *"Am I ready for a loan?"* or *"Can I get credit?"*.
**What Happens:**  
- The system analyzes your last 3 months of recorded transactions.
- It calculates your **Net Cash Flow**.
- It gives a verdict: **"Not Ready"** (with advice on how to improve) or **"Potentially Ready"** (with a score).

### C. Savings Goals
**How to Use:**  
Tell the bot: *"I want to save 1000 cedis for a new freezer"*.
**What Happens:**  
- Creates a tracked Goal in the system.
- The bot encourages you towards this target in future chats.

## 3. Impact Dashboard (Demographics)
**Access:**  
Visit `/impact` (e.g., `osikani.app/impact`)

**Description:**  
A real-time visualization of Osikani's reach across Ghana.

**Features:**
- **Live Counters:** Total Users, Messages Exchanged.
- **Location Map:** Bar chart showing top cities/towns using the app.
- **Gender & Age:** Breakdown of user demographics collected during onboarding.
- **Business Sectors:** See which industries (e.g., Agriculture, Trading, Transport) are being helped most.

---

## 4. Partner Portal (For Banks/MFIs)
**Access:**  
Visit `/partner`

**Description:**  
A portal for financial institutions to identify "credit-ready" users from the informal sector without doing manual groundwork.

**How to Use:**
- **View Candidates:** Partners see a list of anonymous users who meet the "Loan Ready" score criteria.
- **Filter:** Sort by Business Type or Location.
- **Export/Action:** (Simulated) Partners can request to connect with these users to offer micro-loans.

---

## 5. Learning Hub (Gamified Education)
**Access:**  
Visit `/learning` or click "Learning" in the nav.

**Description:**  
A consolidated library of financial modules (e.g., "Savings 101", "Debt Management").

**Features:**
- **Guest Mode:** Start learning immediately without signing up.
- **Progress Tracking:** Tracks which modules you've completed.
- **Quizzes:** Interactive questions to test knowledge after reading.

## 6. Marketplace (Digital Store)
**Access:**

**Description:**  
A simplified e-commerce section for impactful digital resources.

**How to Use:**
